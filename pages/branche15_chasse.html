You approach the outskirts of a village. It is a bit close for comfort but at least you can easily grab a bite and run, you reason. You are still well within the forest but your chances of seeing more than one person are much higher. Your hunger pangs have grown worse, and you can barely stand. You need to do this quickly! Just as your frustration almost gets ahold of you to do something brash, you smell her…


Another young girl walks past you. Does she also have a basket? You cannot see from here.


You shake your head as the thought echoes in your mind.
Focus: food! You feel like you are becoming more and more unhinged.


You recenter yourself and stalk her until she is just out of reach of the sight of the village.



  • You pounce… now!

  • Ugh… just a bit closer, you want to know if she has cake…
