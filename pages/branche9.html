As you approach softly, your eyes are trained on the basket and the child. A twig beneath you snaps under your padded foot. At the sound, the aloof girl startles and turns towards you. “Oh! A wolf! Hello Mr. Wolf!”


Her bright red cloak seemed to float about her as she suddenly twirled about.


“Good day to you, little Red Cap,” you manage with a rasp. You are almost startled at your own voice. You also neglect to correct her: being called Mr. Wolf is fine for now. This is all so absurd.


“What are you carrying?” you continue without expecting an answer. Surely this child must already be enchanted for her to act so strangely to a wolf creature.


You start to remember a child long ago as she recounts how she is carrying snacks to bring to her grandmother; she is unwell and in need of care. Your mind fades as the smell of food overcomes you again…



  • Something about this feels familiar. You believe you know where this house is. Surely this is a good opportunity for enough food to return to full strength! Then you can safely go after the huntsmen! You must find a way to delay the girl.

  • A quick meal with little consequence! You Attack!

  • You've waisted enough time on this child, who tells their child to go alone in the wood anyway!? There could be bears... or worse... huntsmen... You ignore the child and go back to the tracks.
