You arrive at the checkpoint. This will serve as a perfect appetizer to your wrath and voracious hunger. You find a well-hidden spot that affords you a view of the trail and keeps you cloaked within the underbrush. A bestial wolf instinct takes over: a familiar, distinct scent reaches your nose. This is the one that has hunted you over the centuries.
Their scent is so strong it fills you with memories of your battles with a certain knight when the first humans arrived. He had dethroned you…

You remember later quarrels with at least a generation or two of their children. Without thought, the nature of your form takes over: growling and with gnashing teeth, you spring the blood of your enemy as he comes into sight on the trail.

In your vicious and swift attack, your jaws are locked onto their arm when your gaze widens. Gore fills your mouth as you realize that he is not alone. How foolish you were! So blinded by instinct and hunger. But not a complete fool, you did pick a perfect spot after all. It almost felt as if the trees and underbrush were always just in the right spot for you to dash and dodge in between as soon as you let go of your foe.

You are a determined creature, but in your frailty, you are still easily wounded. After putting up much of a fight, it is not enough. You must retreat to fight another day if you can. You manage to run away after doing a great deal of damage to your enemies and destroy the source of your ire. This one looked young, hopefully this is the end of the line for them.

Your body is frail and after many lives, your spirit is weak. You begin to fade, are you satisfied with how this all ends?
