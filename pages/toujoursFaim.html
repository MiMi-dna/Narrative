Still Hungry?

The wolf thought to himself: 'What a tender young creature! what a nice plump mouthful—she will be better to eat than the old woman. I must act craftily, so as to catch both.'
- Little Red-Cap, by The Brothers Grimm



Pastries

Wolf was an old beast and like many of his kind, as old as the wood. This day, unlike many, he found himself especially voracious. A pit sat in his stomach as the smell of fresh baked goods wafted through the air. But there was something else, a bit sweet but not baked. Normally the smell of easy food would not be enough to draw him. It's the something extra that he was curious about. Luckily for him, the scent has not yet made its way to the main road.
