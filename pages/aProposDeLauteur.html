Pastries... It All Started With Pastries...

Erica Rivera is an undergraduate student attending the University of Central Florida for Digital Media with a focus on Video Game Design with a minor in Computer Science.

She is currently undergoing what she hopes to be her final career, her history spans being a pastry chef in fine dining restaurants to being a senior certified pharmacy technician. Erica has experienced a great deal of struggles growing up but she has found herself in a position with great friends and support where she can finally pursue passions. Taking inspiration from her father who is an artist that paints with traditional oil mediums, and her mother who worked in computer infrastructure and facilities managements, Erica endeavors to pursue arts and science in a cumulative way.

Erica wants to take the life lessons from her own history to create art and media that may be able to positively help or inspire others. This is one of her first steps to create a series of stories that will open the door to more opportunities to create and share with her community.
