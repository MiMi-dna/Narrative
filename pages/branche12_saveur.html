You lie in wait, in a bed of your own making with anticipation. It is not long until you catch the whiff of nosegays wafting in through the window. The very flowers the morsel picked in the meadow. You hear the metal latch creak as it is pushed up, just as it did when you had. 


                "“Hello Grandmother! I have come with gifts!... Grandmother?”"

You dare not respond, lest you choke on your own saliva… Just get closer dessert…


With absolute instinct you lunge forward. Did you even chew? You do not remember. But what you do remember finally feeling completely full. How long has it been!?



You are finally ready, you have not felt this strong in ages, it is finally time to hunt the huntsman!
