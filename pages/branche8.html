The smell is too intoxicating, and you are much too hungry. The tracks were easy enough to find the first time so there is no harm in a bit of deviation. If you eat now, you will have some energy for the fight later. You follow the scent and come upon a curious young girl carrying a basket with some wine and cakes. She has not spotted you yet and the hunger pangs stir.



  • Cautiously, you approach, maybe you can talk this silly lone child to give you her snacks...

  • A supple child AND dessert, this must be your luck day! You stalk and attack, you will have both...
