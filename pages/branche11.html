"I think there is a latch…" you say to yourself as you eagerly walk up to the cottage. How could you know that though? What an odd thought. You look up at the door and press against it.

“Someone there?” The withered voice was muffled from within the cabin.

You do your best impression of the little girl: “It is me, grandmother, your little Red Cap, coming to take care of you. Wait until you see what is in my basket. Please help me with this door!”

Woah… You are really good at this, a real natural. This feels just to be on the edge of what you are capable of, but you resist pushing it too far. You cannot risk this meal; you have come much too far. The voice responds:

                “Oh! My little Red Cap, I am too weak to get up. 
                You can open the latch by pressing it up from the outside.
                Remember that I told you, the kind huntsman that came by the other day.
                He helped me change it so that you and your mother could get in more easily. 
                Thankfully it had managed to keep out all the dumb beasts.”

You roll your eyes as you push up on the latch. You walk into an unkempt room. This is what wolves did, this is your nature. To consume the sick and elderly. It was only natural.

You struck swiftly and efficiently until she was all gone. But something does not feel right, you were somehow hungrier. This was not enough… you need more!



  • You need to hunt! Your wolf instincts have been awakened. Leave this dingy room, go back to the checkpoint by the cabin until the girl arrives or something more appetizing comes along…

  • Wait here! The little girl will be your next prey, you know she is coming…
