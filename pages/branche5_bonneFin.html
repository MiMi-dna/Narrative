You have suffered enough and will not take “no” as an answer. You speak, more controlled than ever. Desperate hungry raspiness is replaced by deep wisdom and honeyed promises. Though your form is that of an emaciated wolf, your words reveal your power and promise the lord of fey trickery, the lady of woodland mischief. You remember who you truly are and will remind those foolish trespassers of whose domain they have treaded upon.

All the elder kin bow to you, joining you in your cause. After much deliberation, you and your kin unleash a brutal attack, focusing on the settlements where the huntsmen are most concentrated. While you do not spare those in the cross hairs, you allow the survivors to remain under your rule so long as they engage in the ancient rites and tithes. The wood is safe once more as you and your kin feast well tonight. Balance is restored and you can now rest or rule… The choice is yours now, and no one elses'.
