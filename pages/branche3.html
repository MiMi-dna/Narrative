You will NOT be undone by such an insignificant morsel. With new determination you override the frivolous flesh you inhabit. You are an elder forest spirit, ever present and undying. You will not yield. With new fervor, you stalk further into the forest as memories flood back in. Much of the forest has changed since you were last here but some of it has remained the same.



You happen upon a checkpoint within the wood where the wild trees are still very close to the trail. If your memory serves correctly, the huntsmen frequent this way as it grants cover for friend and foe alike, so long as you are the first to arrive. It might be risky to remain alone though. You remember that beyond a glade further, there is an ancient meeting place for elder forest kin such as yourself used to gather. The memories of ancient tithes at those ancient altars bring your new stomach to growl with anticipation of a new meal. You are closer now than ever…



  • "It's now or never, I can use this as a hold..." you growl as you move into the under brush.

  • You move on, surely if the elder spirits will join you to reclaim the forest...
