The sickeningly sweet aroma lingers on your tongue. Fully satiated, you are finally ready. The memories come flooding in as your ancient powers return. You are not just a great elder spirit, you are the great elder spirit: the lord of fey trickery, the lady of woodland mischief. You are...



You are...



You are tired...



With your ancient hunger finally being abated, you have been satisfied to a degree. However, your recent actions appear to have bound your elder spirit to the weak flesh of an emaciated wolf that has just had its fill. An overwhelming need to rest washes over you and you fall into an unwilling slumber. This is not the sleep your elder kind are used to. This is a much more mortal, simple sleep. Your mind is within a dream that you cannot write in a vessel that will not stir. As you lie asleep, you are approached by the huntsman, who has a very specific smell.

Your immortal mind stirs at the stench but cannot escape as your mortal body dies. Was this the same huntsman who knew of the latch?



  • Your soul was too intertwined with the vessel, you cannot escape...

  • If you remember this scent, then you most certainly know that this man knows of you and what you truly are. He knows that you will simply remain in the forest as long as it lives. You will rest now but you will wake again; it may take years… it may take centuries… You do not know how long, but what is for certain is that you can never truly die.
