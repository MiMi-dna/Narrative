Hmmm…. This is ambitious, even for you.

You have been stirred to perform greater actions before, but this feels different. You would have normally tried to attack alone, and it more, or less, works out in the end, given who you are. But these human pests cannot be tolerated any longer. This feels right. Your pangs of hunger seem trivial compared to the fire that burns within you. Stealthily you skirt the small human settlements along the way until you find the familiar but aged altars of yore...

Home...

You travel deeply into the wild forest until you happen upon the gathering of elder fey. Much like yourself, all in corporeal forms, all burnt embers of the fires that they once were. As their eyes meet your form, you see their faces twist with disgust at your presence. It appears that you are not welcome.
