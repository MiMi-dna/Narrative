Long has it been since your spirit last took form. You forgot what breath was until it took to your lungs, heaving your form up and down. You awake in the darkness of the forest, your home. You are a wolf. You are being hunted. You remember now. With your memories come a deeply unsatisfied hunger. You know you are not safe until the huntsmen have been expelled from this forest. You channel that hatred and hunger to move your large form into the light that falls between the leaves. As you decide to hunt the huntsmen, you catch their scent and see their tracks. Moving forward, you are met with an incredible scent.
