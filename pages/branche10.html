You begin to walk alongside the girl for a moment, you notice as she absent-mindedly walks and just about trips on her own feet every few steps. You both happen upon a meadow where she stops, mouth agape. “Oh, those are her favorite flowers!”


“Now there is a thought. Why, you should take a look and pick a bouquet of the best flowers. Enjoy the song birds as you go. The meadow of our dear wood is a miraculous place!”


You were truly not lying in your reply, but this was the best opportunity to get ahead of the little morsel. Her smell was beginning to be too hard to resist, and you did not know how much longer you could resist.


Time seemed to freeze as the little girl frowned as she stared at the meadow. Finally, she smiled and rushed forward, “if I take a bouquet to grandmother, she would be so pleased. I can surely make it in time,” she giggled as she ran forth. “Goodbye and thank you for the wonderful idea, Mr. Wolf!”


You walk until you are just out of sight and sprint forth. Yes, this feels right! The anticipation of a full belly beckons! Without much thought you find the cottage, the old woman should be inside. It will take the little girl some time to get here. There appears quite a bit of overgrowth around the cottage. You look about and decide how to continue. The hunger pangs increase and it is almost too difficult to stand.



  • Talking through everything seems to be working so far, maybe you can convince grandma to help you with the door. This was you can have the main course, then wait for dessert to deliver itself.

  • If you manage to hide, you can certainly ambush the girl as she goes inside, then you can make a quick meal of both! No more talking, your head hurts! You find a safe enough checkpoint near the trail to the cottage and the overgrowth.
